METHODOLOGY

Why these filters? Across every U.S. stock that returned 100x from 1962–2014, Mayer's study found a consistent signature: small size, ROIC > 15%, sustained 15%+ EPS growth, low debt, and aligned insiders.

10x Score is a weighted composite — growth 30%, quality 25%, reinvestment runway 20%, multiple headroom 10%, size 10%, insider alignment 5%.

Only ~0.7% of Russell 3000 stocks 10x in a decade. The filter is strict on purpose.
